    How the count works

    Output
    Goals and assists from every competitive match; expected goals from 2015. Defenders and keepers: goals conceded.
    Opponents
    Every opponent rated by an Elo computed from 294,000 results; beating strong sides counts more.
    Big games
    Knockouts of the European Cup, World Cup, Euro and Copa América. Finals count triple.
    The window
    Calendar years until 2021, August to July since. 2020 is awarded too.
    Thin data
    Logs thin out before 1993; missing league games are estimated.

    Votes are opinions. This is a count.
